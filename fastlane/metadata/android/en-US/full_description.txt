DAVY is a modern, lightweight open-source client for discovering and watching anime on phones, Android TV, and Google TV. Browse ongoing titles from the current season, search the catalog, explore detailed info, pick your preferred dub/sub and streaming source, and watch seamlessly in the built-in player.

Built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, DAVY adapts its layout to your screen. The mobile interface is crafted for smooth touch gestures, while the TV experience is tailored for D-pad and remote control navigation.

Key Features:

* Phone & Tablet Support: Touch-friendly browsing, fast search, detailed anime pages, and episode tracking.
* TV-Optimized Interface: Clean, responsive layout built specifically for Android TV / Google TV remotes.
* Seasonal Catalog & Search: Stay up to date with the current anime season or find specific titles quickly.
* Flexible Sources: Choose from multiple available translations (dubs/subs) and streaming sources.
* Modern Video Playback: Smooth, reliable streaming powered by Media3 (ExoPlayer) with picture-in-picture and orientation controls.

Note: Streaming sources currently provide content in Russian only.
