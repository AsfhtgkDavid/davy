DAVY is a modern, lightweight media player designed specifically for Android TV and Google TV platforms. It provides a seamless way to discover and stream anime directly on your TV.

Content is currently available in Russian only.

Built from the ground up with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, the app features a highly polished, TV-first interface. Navigate effortlessly using only your remote control (D-pad) without the need for touch emulation.

Key Features:

* TV-Optimized: Focus-based navigation designed specifically for remote controls and smart TVs.
* Fast & Lightweight: Minimum footprint, fast loading times, and smooth UI even on low-end TVs.
* Modern Player: Reliable video streaming built on top of the modern Media3 (ExoPlayer) stack.
