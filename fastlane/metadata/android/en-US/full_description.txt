DAVY is a modern, lightweight open-source app for discovering and watching anime on phones, Android TV, and Google TV. Browse the current season, search for anime, explore detailed information, choose a translation and player, and watch episodes with the built-in video player.

Content is currently available in Russian only.

Built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, DAVY adapts its interface to the device you use. The mobile UI is designed for touch navigation, while the TV UI provides focus-based navigation optimized for remote controls and D-pads.

Key Features:

* Phone Support: Touch-friendly browsing, search, anime details, episode selection, and playback.
* TV-Optimized: Focus-based navigation designed for remote controls and smart TVs.
* Anime Discovery: Browse the current season and find titles with built-in search.
* Flexible Playback: Choose from available translations and players.
* Modern Player: Reliable video streaming powered by Media3 (ExoPlayer).
